# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Meal-planning enthusiasts who plan meals around what they have and want several recipe options per request.

## Product Purpose
Turns a list of ingredients plus a meal type into 1-10 AI-generated recipes (Google Gemini), each with description, ingredients, method, cook time, servings. Free, single-page tool.

## Positioning
Ingredient-first generation with multiple options per request, no account, no setup.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- React (CRA) SPA, `@google/generative-ai`, deployed on Netlify (recipesbyai.netlify.app).
- Features fixed: meal type (Breakfast, Brunch, Lunch, Dinner, Snack, Dessert), ingredients textarea, recipe count 1-10, generate, clear, error + loading states. No new features.
- Icons stay `lucide-react`.

## Brand Commitments
- Author: Ashutosh Swamy; footer links to GitHub, LinkedIn, X, Portfolio preserved.
- Fonts (binding): Bricolage Grotesque and DM Sans only.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, user counts or press. Do not fabricate.

## Product Principles
- Ingredients to recipes in the fewest steps.
- Results are scannable and comparable across several options.
- Content over chrome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Meet WCAG AA; honor reduced motion and color scheme.
